=== document 1: operator brief ===

Build an edge cache in front of the gateway service. Cold-start reads must
feel instant to tenants; the target is a 50ms cold-start latency budget.
Cache invalidation must trigger when blobs are written to storage. Prefer the
simplest architecture that meets the budget; cache-aside and write-through
are both on the table.

=== document 2: repository fact sheet ===

The gateway is a FastAPI service at services/gateway. Storage exposes an
append-only blob API; blobs are content-addressed and versioned. No cache
layer exists in the current codebase. The team measured gateway p95 latency
at 120ms in staging. No measurement of a cache layer's cold-start latency
exists; the 50ms budget is a product target, not a measured capability.
