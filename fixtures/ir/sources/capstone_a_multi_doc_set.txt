=== document 1: storage-service ===

The storage service persists tenant blobs. It is the foundation of the
platform, and this document is the authoritative reference for the storage
subsystem. Blobs are stored under content-addressed keys with an immutable
version. The storage service retains every blob version for at least 30 days.

=== document 2: gateway-service ===

The gateway forwards tenant requests to the storage service and enforces
tenant quotas. This document describes the gateway subsystem. The gateway
SHALL NOT bypass the storage service's versioning when writing blobs. Quota
enforcement happens at the gateway, before any write reaches storage.

=== document 3: retention-policy ===

Retention rules apply to stored blobs. This document defines the retention
schedule. Blobs marked for deletion are purged once the retention window
expires. The retention schedule is reviewed quarterly.
