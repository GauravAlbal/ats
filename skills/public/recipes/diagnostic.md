# Diagnostic recipe (summary)

Full recipe: `docs/ARTIFACT_RECIPES.md` (canonical).

`ASSESS` only.

```text
ASSESS:  observed behavior · expected behavior · evidence
         · competing explanations · causal assessment · confidence
         · insufficiency · recommended next action
```

Hard rule: distinguish **what happened** from **why we believe it happened**.
Causal claims require evidence; absence of evidence is `UNAVAILABLE`, not a
gap to fill.
